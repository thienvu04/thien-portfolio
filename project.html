Classroom Computer Preparation and Deployment

Sarah T. Reed High School • IT Assistant

Completed during my August 2021 – May 2022 role

Project Overview

During my time as an IT Assistant at Sarah T. Reed High School, I helped prepare and distribute more than 50 computers for classrooms and computer carts. The work involved device configuration, labeling, inventory record updates, and deployment to designated locations.

The Need

Computers needed to be configured, labeled, accurately recorded in the inventory, and placed in the correct classrooms or carts. Keeping the equipment organized was important for tracking device availability and location.

Tools & Skills

Google Sheets, device configuration, equipment labeling, inventory tracking, organization, and attention to detail.

My Contribution

As part of the technology support team, my contribution included:

  • Configured and labeled computers before deployment.
  • Updated equipment records using Google Sheets.
  • Helped deploy more than 50 devices to their designated locations.
  • Kept equipment organized so devices could be tracked and located.

Outcome

Our team successfully deployed the devices while maintaining accurate inventory records. My contribution supported the preparation, organization, and distribution of classroom technology.

What I Learned

This experience taught me the importance of organization, accuracy, and attention to detail when managing a large inventory. I learned that preparing devices and maintaining clear records are both important parts of technology support.

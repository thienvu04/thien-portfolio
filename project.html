The need

Classroom computers needed to be configured, labeled, recorded, and placed in the correct classrooms or carts. Accurate inventory records helped the technology team understand where devices were and whether they were available.

My contribution

As part of the technology support team, I helped prepare and distribute more than 50 computers. I also supported an inventory of 300+ computers, updated equipment records, and helped faculty and students use classroom technology.

  1. 01

    Prepare

    Configured and labeled computers before deployment, supporting device setup and imaging.

  2. 02

    Organize

    Updated equipment records in Google Sheets so devices could be tracked and located.

  3. 03

    Deploy & support

    Helped place devices in their designated locations and provided basic technology support.

The outcome

Our team deployed classroom devices while maintaining inventory records. My work supported the preparation, organization, and distribution of technology for staff and students.

Preparing devices and keeping their inventory records up to date helped us find equipment when classrooms needed it.

The work

For an LSU course, I used AdventureWorks sample data to build an interactive sales report in Power BI. I cleaned the data in Power Query and set up model relationships before building the visuals.

What I practiced

  • Cleaning data, replacing missing values, and setting appropriate column types.
  • Creating date-related fields and relationships that support reporting.
  • Combining cards, line charts, maps, and slicers to explore sales by region and time.
  • Adding a Top 10 filter and testing how selecting a sales region changed the report totals.
THE TAKEAWAY

The date and territory relationships let the same filters update the totals, trend chart, and map.
